# Model-Year Risk Research — Highlander, Edge, Explorer

Date: 2026-08-28

## Scope

Add conservative model-year risk records for Toyota Highlander, Ford Edge, and Ford Explorer after those models were added to the initial family search.

## Findings

| Model | Years | Risk | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Toyota Highlander V6 | 2008-2011 | Oil cooler pipe leak/seep and related 2GR-FE oil hose inspection. | Toyota T-SB-0201-11 references 2008-2011 Highlander oil cooler pipe leak/seep; NHTSA/TSB mirrors also reference limited service campaign/warranty enhancement material for VVT-i/oil cooler pipes. |
| Toyota Highlander | 2014-2015 | Power back door may stop or become inoperative. | Toyota T-SB-0023-16 / T-SB-0027-15 summaries describe 2014-2015 Highlander and Highlander HV power back door stoppage/inoperative conditions. |
| Toyota Highlander | Certain 2015 vehicles | Electric power steering ECU recall applicability must be checked by VIN. | NHTSA campaign 15V144000 covers certain 2015 Highlander/Highlander Hybrid vehicles; remedy is inspection/replacement for affected EPS ECU/steering column serial range. |
| Ford Edge 2.0L EcoBoost | 2015-2018 | Coolant intrusion into cylinders can require long-block replacement. | Ford TSB 19-2208 / 22-2229 summaries describe low coolant, white exhaust smoke, rough running, misfire/overheat DTCs, and long-block replacement for affected 2.0L EcoBoost vehicles. |
| Ford Edge | 2015-2018 | Brake hose rupture recall/investigation history; verify completed recall and current brake-hose condition. | NHTSA/Ford recall 20V469 covered front brake hoses; NHTSA ODI PE24027 later investigated rear brake hose ruptures in 2015-2017 Edge and led to recall 25V544 according to investigation summaries. |
| Ford Explorer | 2011-2017 | Exhaust odor / carbon monoxide concern; verify program completion and collision/upfit history. | Ford customer satisfaction programs 17N03/19N05 and NHTSA investigation summaries cover 2011-2017 Explorer carbon monoxide/exhaust odor concerns. |
| Ford Explorer | 2013-2017 | Rear suspension toe-link inspection/recall applicability. | NHTSA/Ford recall/program summaries for 20V675, 21S32/21B14, and related communications cover toe-link fracture/inspection risks on affected 2013-2017 Explorer vehicles, with stronger concern in corrosion regions and certain repaired populations. |

## Decision

Seed these as caution/neutral inspectable risks rather than broad exclusions. VIN checks, service records, and pre-purchase inspection should reduce concern when repairs are documented.
